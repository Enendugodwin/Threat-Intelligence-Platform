In this article ScreenConnect architecture and attack surfaces Attack chain overview Mitigation and protection guidance Indicators of compromise and supporting artifacts References Learn More Beginning on November 11, 2026, ConnectWise reported an active campaign of a threat actor gaining unauthorized access to cloud-hosted ConnectWise ScreenConnect instances belonging to managed service providers (MSPs). Microsoft Threat Intelligence has confirmed ConnectWise’s reported activity and we have also observed threat actors abusing ScreenConnect, a legitimate remote-support application, to install unauthorized remote-access clients and deploy a malicious worm, SCKit, on ConnectWise customer managed endpoints. Additionally, Microsoft analyzed the malicious worm’s behavior: deployment starts with commands issued through ScreenConnect to silently install an identical client connected to threat actor-controlled infrastructure. A batch launcher retrieves a PowerShell decoder that decrypts and extracts SCKit using device-specific key material. The toolkit establishes persistence through scheduled tasks and registry Run entries and deploys RelayTrap, a component designed to deliver a malicious payload to a connecting technician. We have identified that the worm targets IT support technician workstations using the hijacked authenticated ScreenConnect Session and uses the victim’s management console to distribute payloads to other managed devices. The resulting access to either target enables threat actors to remotely administer affected endpoints, execute commands, and deploy further tools independently of the legitimate support instance. Defenders should investigate technician workstations and downstream affected endpoints. Removing an unauthorized client alone does not remove the toolkit’s persistence or invalidate exposed web sessions. In this blog, we examine SCKit’s attack chain and payload components and provide mitigation guidance, indicators of compromise, and Microsoft Defender XDR advanced hunting queries to help organizations identify and disrupt this activity. ScreenConnect architecture and attack surfaces ScreenConnect, formerly ConnectWise Control, is a remote-support and access platform used by MSPs to administer customer devices, often alongside remote monitoring and management (RMM) software.
